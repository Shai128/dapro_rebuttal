\subsection{Budget validity of the standard CRC controller}
\label{sec:standard_crc_budget_validity}

We prove the claim in~\eqref{eq:dapro_main_budget_guarantee}.  Condition on
the fully observed policy-fit fold $\calIone$.  After conditioning, the
candidate family $\{P_\lambda:\lambda\in[0,1]\}$, its cumulative-reach cap,
and $B_{\mathrm{rem}}$ are fixed.  Let $Z$ denote a complete trajectory and
write
\[
    b(Z):=\min\{T,Q(X)\},
    \qquad
    c(Z;\lambda):=\sum_{t=1}^{b(Z)}\rho_Z^\lambda(t),
    \qquad
    \ell(Z;\lambda):=c(Z;\lambda)+\frac{n}{m}b(Z).
\]
By construction,
\begin{equation}
    0\leq\ell(Z;\lambda)\leq
    L_{\max}:=C_{\max}+\frac{n}{m}\maxl
    \label{eq:standard_crc_loss_bound}
\end{equation}
for every trajectory in the population support and every candidate.  Let
$a:=B_{\mathrm{rem}}/m$ and define
\begin{equation}
    \widehat\lambda
    :=\sup\left\{\lambda:
       \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\ell(Z_i;\lambda)+L_{\max}}
            {n+1}
       \leq a
    \right\}.
    \label{eq:standard_crc_appendix_selector}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Marginal expected total-budget validity]
\label{prop:standard_crc_budget_validity}
Suppose, conditional on $\calIone$, that: (i) the CRC trajectories
$Z_1,\ldots,Z_n$ and a fresh deployment trajectory $Z_{n+1}$ are
exchangeable; (ii) the complete candidate family is fixed independently of
$Z_1,\ldots,Z_n$; (iii) $\ell(z;\lambda)$ is measurable,
right-continuous, and non-decreasing in $\lambda$; and (iv) the selector in
\eqref{eq:standard_crc_appendix_selector} and its augmented-sample analogue
are feasible.  Then
\[
    m\,\mathbb E[c(Z_{n+1};\widehat\lambda)\mid\calIone]
    +n\,\mathbb E[b(Z_{n+1})\mid\calIone]
    \leq B_{\mathrm{rem}}.
\]
Consequently, after adding the fixed policy-fit expenditure, the expected
total cost of policy fitting, full CRC observation, and randomized deployment
does not exceed $B_{\mathrm{tot}}$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Suppress conditioning on $\calIone$.  Add the fresh trajectory to the CRC
sample and define the symmetric augmented selector
\[
    \widetilde\lambda
    :=\sup\left\{\lambda:
      \frac{1}{n+1}\sum_{i=1}^{n+1}\ell(Z_i;\lambda)
      \leq a
    \right\}.
\]
The support bound~\eqref{eq:standard_crc_loss_bound} implies
\[
    \sum_{i=1}^{n}\ell(Z_i;\widehat\lambda)
    +\ell(Z_{n+1};\widehat\lambda)
    \leq
    \sum_{i=1}^{n}\ell(Z_i;\widehat\lambda)+L_{\max}
    \leq(n+1)a.
\]
Thus $\widehat\lambda$ is feasible for the augmented problem and
$\widetilde\lambda\geq\widehat\lambda$.  Monotonicity gives
\[
    \ell(Z_{n+1};\widehat\lambda)
    \leq\ell(Z_{n+1};\widetilde\lambda).
\]
Because $\widetilde\lambda$ is a permutation-invariant function of the
$n+1$ exchangeable trajectories,
\begin{align*}
    \mathbb E[\ell(Z_{n+1};\widetilde\lambda)]
    &=\mathbb E\!\left[
       \frac{1}{n+1}\sum_{i=1}^{n+1}
       \ell(Z_i;\widetilde\lambda)
      \right]\\
    &\leq a.
\end{align*}
Therefore
\[
    \mathbb E[c(Z_{n+1};\widehat\lambda)]
    +\frac{n}{m}\mathbb E[b(Z_{n+1})]
    \leq\frac{B_{\mathrm{rem}}}{m}.
\]
Multiplying by $m$ proves the displayed claim.  The $n$ CRC rows have the
same expected full-observation cost as $n$ fresh rows.  Finally, conditional
on a complete deployment trajectory, the realized randomized acquisition
cost has expectation $c(Z;\widehat\lambda)$; adding the fixed policy-fit cost
completes the proof.
\end{proof}

The argument is unchanged for the finite grid used by the implementation:
replace every supremum by the largest feasible grid element.

\paragraph{What the proposition does not imply.}
The proposition is marginal over CRC rows, deployment rows, and acquisition
randomness.  It does not imply a conditional budget bound for each realized
split, and it provides no lower bound on budget utilization.  Conservatism
comes from replacing the unobserved augmented loss by $L_{\max}$, from a
possibly binding row cap, and from discretizing the contraction parameter.
